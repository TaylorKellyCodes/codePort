The Person Behind the Work

A Raleigh Web Designer Who Builds the Sites Himself

Triangle Web Design is a one-person shop based in Raleigh, North Carolina. I'm Taylor Kelly — the person who answers the phone, writes the code, and runs the SEO. There is no account manager between you and the work. If something needs to change on your site, you talk to the person who built it.

I started Triangle Web Design after working at another agency and taking on freelance web design clients across the Triangle. Agency life taught me how projects actually get delivered. Freelance work taught me what small business owners in Raleigh, Durham, and Chapel Hill actually need: a fast site, a clear next step for the visitor, and someone who will still pick up the phone six months later.

Before this, I trained as a software engineer through 100Devs and earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Western Governors University. That engineering background is why every Triangle Web Design site is hand-coded instead of assembled in WordPress or Wix. Templates are convenient for the designer. They are slow, fragile, and expensive for the business paying for them.

  • Based in Raleigh, Serving the Triangle

    I live and work in Raleigh. Client meetings happen here, in Durham, in Chapel Hill, or over a screen share — whichever is easier for you. Local means I already know how search behaves differently on Capital Boulevard versus Franklin Street versus the Bull City.

  • Open Source Community, Not Just Client Work

    From 2022 to 2025 I volunteered with All Things Open, one of the largest open-source technology conferences in the U.S., supporting logistics and attendee engagement for 5,000+ participants. I also volunteered at DevOpsDays Raleigh. That community work is how I stay current — and why I care about clean, maintainable code.

  • A Short Client List on Purpose

    I keep a small roster so every account gets real attention. You will not be handed off to a junior after the contract is signed. The $0-down, $150/month model exists so Triangle small businesses can get a custom site without a $5,000 invoice on day one.

    Sites are written in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No page builder bloat, no plugin updates that break the homepage overnight, and load times that actually pass Google's Core Web Vitals.

    Software engineering training through 100Devs and a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Western Governors University shape how I build: version-controlled code, accessible markup, and sites that a future developer can actually maintain.

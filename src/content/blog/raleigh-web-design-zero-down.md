## How Does Monthly Pricing Compare With a Large Upfront Build?

A monthly plan costs less to start and spreads the price over time. Many agencies charge several thousand dollars before the site goes live. That is a real risk for a small business, because the money is spent before you know whether the site brings in calls.

With a monthly plan the provider has a reason to keep the site working well. The site has to keep performing for you to keep paying.

## Why Is a Hand-Coded Site Better Than Wix or WordPress?

For a local service company, a hand-coded site is usually faster and easier to keep clean. Each page is written for your business instead of assembled from a template with extra plugins. There is less code for the browser to load, so pages appear sooner.

Speed matters because slow pages lose visitors before they call. Google also measures page experience, including how quickly the main content loads. You can test any site for free with Google's [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/).

Template platforms suit some businesses well, especially owners who want to edit pages themselves every day. If you depend on search and phone calls and do not want to maintain a site, speed and clean structure count for extra. Read the full comparison in [hand-coded vs WordPress](/blog/hand-coded-vs-wordpress/), and see why speed matters in [website speed and mobile-first design](/blog/website-speed-mobile-first-design/).

## What Should Raleigh Web Design Include to Bring In Calls?

A good Raleigh web design project is built around the phone, not around decoration. A visitor on a phone should reach you in one tap and understand what you do within a few seconds.

These are the parts that matter most for a local service business:

* A tap-to-call phone number at the top of every page
* A separate page for each core service, so each one can rank on its own
* Clear mention of the towns you serve, with real detail
* Reviews and project photos that show the work
* Fast loading on mobile, tested before launch
* Your name, address, and phone number written the same way as on your Google Business Profile

None of these needs a large budget. They need care, and that is where hand-coding helps. We can place each element exactly where it works best, and nothing extra slows the page down. Pair the site with a complete Google Business Profile and the two support each other.

## What Should You Ask Before You Sign?

Ask any web designer these questions, including us:

1. What exactly is included in the monthly price?
2. How many pages are included, and what does each extra page cost?
3. Who handles edits, and how fast do they happen?
4. Who owns the site, and what happens if I stop paying?
5. How will you measure page speed before launch?

Clear answers to these tell you as much as a portfolio does. A good provider answers them in writing and does not rush you.

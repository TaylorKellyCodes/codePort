A free website SEO audit is a review of how your site performs and how well it is set up to bring in local customers. You receive a written summary and a prioritized list of fixes, whether or not you hire the company that ran it. This post explains how the audit works at Triangle Web Design.

The goal is a clear picture. You should finish knowing what is working, what is not, and what to do first.

## What Does a Free Website SEO Audit Cover?

We review seven areas:

* **Performance.** Load time and Core Web Vitals on mobile and desktop, using real field data where it is available.
* **Mobile experience.** Tap targets, readable text, a visible phone number, and forms that are easy to finish on a phone.
* **Local SEO setup.** Your Google Business Profile categories, services, service area, hours, photos, and review activity.
* **On-page SEO.** Title tags, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, internal links, and whether you have service and location pages that match how people search.
* **Structured data.** LocalBusiness, FAQ, and Review markup, which gives search engines and AI tools clear facts to work from.
* **Technical health.** Your sitemap, robots file, canonical tags, HTTPS, and anything that blocks pages from search.
* **Content gaps.** Searches your competitors appear for that you do not, and the pages that would close the gap.

Slow pages cost rankings and calls, so performance is often where the quickest wins are. Google explains the main speed measures in its [Core Web Vitals guide](https://web.dev/articles/vitals), and our post on [website speed and mobile-first design](/blog/website-speed-mobile-first-design/) covers the fixes.

## Why Run a Free Website SEO Audit First?

An audit shows you where the problem is before you spend money on a fix. Many owners guess. They assume they need a new site when the real issue is a profile category, or they pay for ads when the site cannot turn visitors into calls.

A free website SEO audit replaces the guess with a list. It also gives you a neutral benchmark. If you hire anyone later, you can compare their plan against the findings and ask why a recommendation is not on the list.

Common findings in local service businesses include:

* Pages that load slowly on a phone
* A phone number that is text, not a tappable link
* Service pages that combine several services in one
* A Google Business Profile with the wrong primary category
* Business details that differ between the site and directories

Each of these is fixable, and most are cheaper than owners expect. Several can be corrected in a single afternoon once you know they exist, which is exactly what the written list is for.

## How Long Does a Free SEO Audit Take?

The review takes a few business days. Walking you through the results takes 20 to 30 minutes, by phone or video, at a time that suits you.

The walkthrough covers the findings and gives you a chance to ask questions.

## What Do You Get From the Audit?

You leave with four things:

1. A written summary of what is working and what needs attention
2. A prioritized list, with the highest-impact fixes first
3. A clear view of which items you or your current provider can handle, and which need development work
4. Straight answers about cost and timeline

The list separates items you or your current provider can handle from those that need development work, so you can decide what to do yourself and what to hand off.

There is no obligation. If your site is in good shape, we tell you so. If it has problems worth fixing, you have a plan whether you fix them yourself, hand the list to your current provider, or ask us to help.

## What Happens After the Audit?

You choose what to do next. Some owners fix the small items themselves in an afternoon, such as hours, categories, and photos. Others hand the list to their current web provider. Some ask us to build the fixes.

Whichever route you take, keep the written list. It gives you something concrete to compare against any quote you receive later, and it lets you check the work once it is done. Work from the top of the list. The highest-impact items come first because they usually move rankings and calls the most, and the lower items can wait until the basics are in place.

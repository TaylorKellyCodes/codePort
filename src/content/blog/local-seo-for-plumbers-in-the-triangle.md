A homeowner in Cary walks into the kitchen at 6 AM and hears water running where it should be quiet. Within a minute they have their phone out, and they are typing "emergency plumber near me." Whoever shows up first with a phone number, a service area they recognize, and a few recent reviews is the one who gets the call.

That search happens hundreds of times a day across the Triangle, for burst pipes, water heaters, clogged mains, and slab leaks. Local SEO is how a plumbing company makes sure it is the business those searchers see and trust. This guide covers the parts that reliably produce booked jobs, in the order worth tackling them.

## Start With the Google Business Profile

For a plumber, the Google Business Profile is the single highest-leverage asset in local search. It feeds Google Maps, the local pack that sits above the regular results, and the AI summaries Google now shows for "plumber near me" style queries.

### Fill Out Every Field

A complete profile gives Google more reasons to show you and gives the homeowner fewer reasons to hesitate:

* Set the primary category to "Plumber." Add secondary categories that match real services, such as "Drainage service," "Water tank cleaning service," or "Hot water system supplier."
* List your services individually with short descriptions. "Water heater replacement," "Sewer line repair," and "Sump pump installation" each help you match specific searches.
* Add your hours, and mark whether you offer 24-hour emergency service. If you do after-hours work, say so clearly, because that phrase is exactly what late-night searchers look for.
* Set a service area built from the towns and ZIP codes you actually cover: Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Morrisville, Durham, Wake Forest, Garner, and the rest of your real footprint.

### Keep It Active

Profiles that get updated tend to hold stronger positions. Post a project photo once a week, refresh your seasonal messaging (frozen pipe prevention in January, irrigation and outdoor spigots in spring), and upload real photos of your trucks, your team, and finished work. Twenty or more genuine photos give both Google and the homeowner a fuller picture of the business.

## Build Reviews That Do More Than Add Stars

Reviews influence where you rank and whether someone picks you once they find you. A few habits make the difference.

1. **Ask every satisfied customer, the same day.** Send a text with a direct link to your Google review form while the job is fresh.
2. **Encourage specifics.** A review that says "replaced our water heater in Holly Springs the same afternoon" helps you rank for that service and that town. You cannot script reviews, but you can tell customers what was helpful to hear.
3. **Respond to all of them.** Thank the positive reviewers by name and service. Answer the critical ones calmly with what you did to make it right. Both responses show future customers how you operate.
4. **Keep a steady pace.** Ten reviews across the last two months signal an active, in-demand business more than fifty reviews from three years ago.

## Give Every Service Its Own Page

Homeowners and search engines both reward depth. A single "Services" page with a bulleted list gives Google very little to match against. Individual pages give you a place to rank for each high-intent search.

Pages worth building for a Triangle plumbing company:

* Emergency plumbing repair
* Water heater repair and replacement (include tankless)
* Drain cleaning and clog removal
* Sewer line inspection and repair
* Repiping and leak detection
* Fixture installation (faucets, toilets, garbage disposals)
* Well pump and pressure tank service, which matters in the more rural parts of Wake, Durham, and Chatham counties

On each page, cover what the service includes, how long it usually takes, a realistic price range or starting price, the neighborhoods you serve it in, and two or three questions customers actually ask. A short FAQ near the bottom of the page gives you another way to match voice searches and AI answers.

## Create Location Pages for the Towns You Serve

If you work across several Triangle towns, a dedicated page for each key market helps you show up when someone adds a place name to their search. "Plumber in Apex" and "Durham drain cleaning" are easier to win with a page built for that town than with a homepage that mentions everywhere at once.

A strong location page includes local details: neighborhoods and subdivisions you frequently work in, notes on common plumbing issues in that area (older cast iron in parts of Durham, newer PEX systems in Wake Forest builds), and reviews from customers who live there. Keep the writing specific to the town so each page stands on its own.

## Get the Website Fundamentals Right

Local rankings and booked jobs both depend on a site that loads fast and works on a phone.

* **Speed.** Most emergency plumbing searches happen on mobile data. A site that loads in two seconds keeps the visitor with you. Hand-coded pages without heavy page builders and plugins make this easier to sustain.
* **Click-to-call.** Your phone number belongs in the header as a tappable link, visible without scrolling on every page.
* **Consistent name, address, and phone.** Match your business name, address, and phone number exactly across your site, your Google profile, Yelp, Angi, the BBB, and local directories. Matching details build trust with Google's local algorithm and with AI tools that cross-check sources before recommending a business.
* **LocalBusiness schema.** Add structured data that states your name, service area, hours, and phone in a format search engines read directly. Adding FAQ schema to your service pages makes those answers eligible for rich results.

## Earn Local Links and Citations

Links and mentions from around the Triangle tell Google you are an established part of the community.

* Join the local chamber of commerce and make sure your listing is complete.
* Get listed in reputable plumbing and home service directories with matching details.
* Sponsor a youth sports team, a school fundraiser, or a neighborhood event, and ask for a link from the organizer's site.
* Partner with related trades, such as remodelers and HVAC companies, who can refer work and link to you.

A handful of real local links carries more weight than a large batch of generic directory submissions.

## A Simple Order of Operations

If you are starting close to scratch, this sequence tends to produce results fastest:

1. Claim and fully complete the Google Business Profile.
2. Set up a same-day review request for every job.
3. Build or rewrite your top three service pages with real detail.
4. Fix site speed and add click-to-call and schema.
5. Add location pages for your top three towns.
6. Build local links steadily, a few each month.

Local SEO for plumbers rewards consistency more than any single trick. The companies that own the Map Pack in Raleigh, Durham, and Chapel Hill are the ones that keep their profile current, ask for reviews on every job, and publish pages that answer real questions.
